Support

Answers first, then a direct line.

Most questions are answered below. Anomaly Hunter is a free project run by one person, so there is no reply-time promise.

QuestionAnswer
The pane says "The checker isn't running"

Wait 30 seconds, then press Try again. Still stuck? Double-click AnomalyHunter.exe again. It asks you to close Excel, repairs the install, starts the checker and opens Excel again.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

The app is not code-signed yet, so Windows warns about it. Click More info, then Run anyway. To check the file first, compare its SHA-256 with the one on the release page.

How do I add it to Excel?

After the install, Excel opens a "Start here" workbook with the steps: Home › Add-ins › More Add-ins › SHARED FOLDER › Anomaly Hunter › Add. You do this once.

How do I update?

Download the latest AnomalyHunter.exe and double-click it. If Excel is open, it asks you to close it first. Your AI key stays. The version you have is the Engine line at the bottom of the pane.

How do I uninstall?

Press Windows+R, paste "%LOCALAPPDATA%\AnomalyHunter\AnomalyHunter.exe" uninstall and press Enter. It removes the add-in, the checker, its certificate and its files, except the app file itself, which you can then delete. It is not listed in Windows "Installed apps". The privacy page lists exactly what it removes.

Where is AI help, and what does Remove key do?

Open AI help (optional, free) at the bottom of the pane. Paste a free Google AI key from aistudio.google.com/apikey, or an invite code (it starts with inv_), and click Save key. Then use Ask AI on a flagged row.

Remove key deletes the key and invite code saved in your AnomalyHunter folder; scans and fixes keep working. With an invite kit, also delete invite-code.txt from the kit folder, or installing again turns AI help back on.

It flagged a correct value, or missed one

It is a helper, not a guarantee, so some false alarms and misses happen. Move the How hard to look slider: lower for fewer, surer flags; higher to look harder. A suggestion changes nothing until you click Accept; Dismiss skips it.

Is my data private?

A scan runs on your PC and sends nothing. Only Ask AI and Check words with AI send part of your sheet: to Google, or through John's relay to Google if you use an invite code. The privacy page says exactly what.
