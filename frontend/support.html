Frequently asked

Quick answers

The pane says "the checker isn't running"
The local engine hasn't started. Double-click install.bat in your Anomaly Hunter folder, then press Try again in the pane. After any update, close Excel first and run install.bat again so the engine restarts.
How do I turn on AI help?
Open AI help (optional, free) at the bottom of the pane, create a free Google AI key at aistudio.google.com/apikey (no credit card), paste it, and click Save. It is stored only on your PC and can be removed anytime.
It flagged a value that's correct, or missed one
Anomaly Hunter is a helper, not a guarantee, so a few false alarms and misses are normal. Use the How hard to look slider: lower it for fewer, higher-confidence flags; raise it to cast a wider net. Nothing in your sheet changes until you review a suggestion and click Apply.
Is my spreadsheet data private?
Yes. Everything runs on your own PC and nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on AI help. See the full Privacy Policy for the details.
How do I update or uninstall?
To update, get the latest release and re-run install.bat (close Excel first). To uninstall, run AnomalyHunter.exe uninstall or remove it from Windows "Installed apps": this also deletes the local files listed on the Privacy page.
