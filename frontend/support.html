Common questions

The pane says "The checker isn't running"
Wait 30 seconds, then press Try again. If that doesn't help, double-click Arcas.exe again. It asks you to close Excel, repairs the install, starts the checker and opens Excel again.
Windows says "Windows protected your PC"
The app is not code-signed yet, so Windows warns about it. Click More info, then Run anyway.
How do I update?
Download the latest Arcas.exe and double-click it. If Excel is open, it asks you to close it first. Your AI key stays. The version you have is the Engine line at the bottom of the pane.
How do I uninstall?
Press Windows+R, paste "%LOCALAPPDATA%\AnomalyHunter\Arcas.exe" uninstall and press Enter. It removes the add-in, the checker, its certificate and its files, except the app file itself, which you can then delete. It is not listed in Windows "Installed apps". The Privacy page lists what it removes.
Where is AI help, and what does Remove key do?
Open AI help (optional, free) at the bottom of the pane. Paste a free Google AI key from aistudio.google.com/apikey, or an invite code (it starts with inv_), and click Save key. Then use Ask AI on a flagged row. Remove key deletes the key and invite code saved in your AnomalyHunter folder; scans and fixes keep working. With an invite kit, also delete invite-code.txt from the kit folder, or installing again turns AI help back on.
It flagged a correct value, or missed one
It can flag correct values and miss mistakes. Move the How hard to look slider: lower for fewer, surer flags; higher to look harder. A suggestion changes nothing until you click Accept; Dismiss skips it.
Is my data private?
A scan runs on your PC and sends nothing. Only Ask AI and Check words with AI send part of your sheet: to Google, or through John's relay to Google if you use an invite code. The Privacy page says what is sent.

Ask John

Never paste real customer data, account numbers, your AI key or your invite code: use made-up values. Copy the Engine line from the bottom of the pane into your message. The only public ways to reach John are the buttons on this page (with an invite, you can also use the way you got it). He will never ask for your key, invite code, password or file.
