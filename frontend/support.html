Common questions

"The checker isn't running"

The local engine isn't started. Double-click install.bat in your Anomaly Hunter folder, then press Try again in the pane. After any update, close Excel first and run install.bat again so the engine restarts.

How do I turn on AI help?

Open AI help (optional, free) at the bottom of the pane, create a free Google AI key at aistudio.google.com/apikey (no credit card), paste it, and click Save. You can remove it anytime.

It flagged a value that's actually fine (or missed one)

Anomaly Hunter is a helper, not a guarantee — some false alarms and misses are normal. Use the How hard to look slider: lower it for fewer, higher-confidence flags; raise it to cast a wider net. Nothing changes in your sheet until you review a suggestion and click Apply.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs on your own PC and nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on AI help. Full detail is on the Privacy & Terms page.

How do I uninstall?

Run AnomalyHunter.exe uninstall (or remove it from Windows "Installed apps"). That also deletes the local files listed on the Privacy page.

I still see the old version after updating

Close Excel completely, re-run install.bat, then reopen the pane. If a style looks stale, reopen the task pane to clear the cache.
