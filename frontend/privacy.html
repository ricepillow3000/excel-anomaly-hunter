The short version. A scan runs on your PC and sends nothing to the internet. Your sheet's data leaves your PC only when you click Ask AI or Check words with AI, and then only the part described below. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and the add-in sets no cookies.

What stays on your PC

The scan

Excel hands your sheet to a small engine on your own PC, at https://127.0.0.1:5055. It checks the sheet in memory and answers the pane. A scan never sends anything to the internet.

Files in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnomalyHunter

  • AnomalyHunter.exe: a copy of the app, so the engine can start when you log on.
  • server.log: the engine's messages and errors. It starts empty each time the engine starts. An error message can quote a value from your sheet.
  • ai-cache: up to 500 AI answers, so the same question isn't asked twice. An answer can quote your cells.
  • gemini-key.txt or invite-code.txt: your Google AI key or invite code, if you added one.
  • ca.pem, cert.pem, key.pem: the certificate the engine uses for HTTPS on this PC.
  • addin\manifest.xml: the file that lists Anomaly Hunter in Excel.
  • Start here.xlsx: the practice workbook.
  • anomaly-hunter.pbids: a shortcut that opens the Power BI file in Power BI.
  • latest-scan.csv: only if you turn on "Save each scan for Power BI" (off unless you turn it on). It holds a plain-text copy of every cell of the last sheet you scanned, plus each row's result. Each scan replaces it. After you turn the option off, the next scan deletes it.

In your workbook

The workbook you scan keeps the limits you changed, the strength slider, a list of the last auto-fixes with their old values (so Undo works), and a setting that opens the pane with that file. Highlights are fill colours written into your cells. A fix changes your cells only when you click Accept, or when auto-fix runs, if you turned auto-fix on.

In the pane

The auto-fix switch and the Power BI switch are saved in Excel's storage for the pane, on this PC.

What AI help sends

AI help works only after you paste a Google AI key or an invite code, or install an invite kit from John. Even then, nothing from your sheet is sent until you click Ask AI or Check words with AI.

When you save a Google key, the engine asks Google once whether the key works. That call carries the key and no sheet data.

  • Ask AI sends the column names, the row you asked about, up to 5 rows above and 5 below it, the formulas in that row, the cell addresses, the reason it was flagged, and anything you type in the request box. On a wide sheet, only the columns nearest the flagged cell go. Long cells are cut to 200 characters, except the flagged cell.
  • Check words with AI sends the names of your text columns that have 2 to 50 different values, and those different values, up to 60 characters each. It never sends whole rows or numbers. These values can include names of people or places.

Where it goes

  • With your own Google key: straight to Google's Gemini API.
  • With an invite code: to John's relay on Cloudflare, which passes it to Google with John's key.
  • With neither, but with ANTHROPIC_API_KEY set on your PC (a developer setting): to Anthropic's Claude.

Google's free tier may use what you send to improve its products. Invite codes always use John's free key. With your own key, it depends on your Google plan. Don't ask AI about private or personal data.

Who else sees something

  • Google receives what AI help sends, under Google's own terms.
  • Cloudflare and John's relay, only with an invite code. Each AI request passes through it. The relay keeps one counter for today: how many AI calls each invite made, under the name John gave that invite, plus the total. It saves no request and no answer, and its saved logs are switched off. The list of invites (a scrambled copy of each code and a name) stays until John removes it. Cloudflare sees your IP address as the request passes.
  • Anthropic, only in the developer case above.
  • GitHub hosts the download, so GitHub sees your IP address when you download the app. Anything you post in a GitHub issue is public.
  • Microsoft: Excel loads Microsoft's Office script from Microsoft for the pane, and Windows may check the downloaded file. Microsoft's privacy terms apply.

Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for ads or tracking.

Your control

  • Turn off AI help: click Remove key in the pane. It deletes your key and invite code from this PC. Scans and fixes keep working.
  • Delete the AI cache or the log: delete ai-cache or server.log in the folder above, or uninstall.
  • Uninstall: press Windows+R, paste "%LOCALAPPDATA%\AnomalyHunter\AnomalyHunter.exe" uninstall and press Enter. It removes the startup entry, the Excel add-in listing, the certificate and the files in that folder, except the app file itself, which you can then delete. It also clears Excel's add-in cache, so other add-ins you added from a shared folder must be added again. It never touches your workbooks.
  • Your invite: ask John, the way you got the invite, to remove it.
  • A GitHub issue: you can edit or delete your own. John can delete it if you ask. Copies made while it was public may remain.

John holds no other data about you.

Security

  • The engine answers only on 127.0.0.1, over HTTPS. Install adds a certificate to your Windows account that works only for localhost and 127.0.0.1. The key that made it is never saved, so it can't sign anything else.
  • Websites can't call the engine: it checks where each request comes from. Other programs on this PC can, like any local service.
  • The pane loads only its own files and Microsoft's Office script. Its Content Security Policy blocks everything else. The fonts are on your PC.
  • Install also adds a startup entry, so the engine runs when you log on, and a trusted add-in folder for Excel. Uninstall removes both.
