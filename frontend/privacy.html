Introduction

Welcome to Anomaly Hunter, a free Microsoft Excel add-in that scans your spreadsheet for likely data problems and suggests fixes. This Privacy Policy explains what information is and is not involved when you use it.

Anomaly Hunter is an independent project and is not affiliated with Microsoft or Google. By installing or using it, you agree to this policy. It was designed around a single principle: your data stays with you.

Information We Collect

Information you provide

Almost nothing. There is no sign-up and no account. The only data you may provide is an optional AI key or invite code, which you paste into the pane. It is stored only on your own PC (in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnomalyHunter) and is never transmitted to us. If you contact Support, you provide whatever you choose to include in that message.

Information collected automatically

None. Anomaly Hunter has no analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixels, and no telemetry. The pane loads only its own files plus Microsoft's Office script, and its Content Security Policy blocks everything else. Your spreadsheet contents are processed in memory by a local engine at https://127.0.0.1:5055 that only your own computer can reach.

Third-party data

Only if you deliberately turn on AI help. In that case a minimal slice of the flagged row (or the distinct words of your text columns) is sent to Google's Gemini API, governed by Google's own privacy terms. Nothing is sent to any other third party, ever.

How We Use Your Information

Because the work happens on your machine, the limited data involved is used only to make the tool function:

  • Scanning — your sheet is checked for anomalies locally and the results shown in the pane.
  • Suggesting fixes — proposed corrections are computed from your own data; nothing changes until you click Apply.
  • Optional AI fixes — if enabled, the minimum context is sent to Google to draft a suggested fix.
  • Your AI key — used solely to authenticate your own requests to Google, from your PC.

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, profiling, model training, or resale. There is no behavioural tracking of any kind.

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Full stop. We also do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics companies, because we never collect it in the first place.

The only circumstances in which any data leaves your PC are:

  • Optional AI help you enable — sent to Google (with your own key) or through the project's Cloudflare relay (with an invite code). The relay keeps only a daily usage count and stores none of your content.
  • Legal requirement — if we were ever legally compelled, we could only disclose what we hold, which is nothing about you.

Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California, you have rights to access, correct, delete, and opt out of the sale of your personal data, and to not be discriminated against for exercising them.

Anomaly Hunter makes these straightforward, because we hold no personal data about you on any server:

  • Access & portability — all data lives on your own PC; you can open it directly in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnomalyHunter.
  • Deletion — click Remove key in the pane, or run AnomalyHunter.exe uninstall, which deletes every local file.
  • Opt out of sale — nothing is ever sold, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • AI opt-out — AI help is off by default and removable anytime.

To ask a question or make a formal request, use the Support page.

Data Security & Retention

Security. The local engine is reachable only from your own computer over HTTPS, using a certificate that is valid for 127.0.0.1 only. A strict Content Security Policy blocks any outside code or connection. Because there is no central server holding user data, there is no central database to breach.

Retention. We retain nothing. The only stored data are the local files you control: an optional AI key, a capped cache of up to 500 finished AI answers, an error log, and — only if you enable it — a single Power BI export of your last scan. Each is deleted when you remove it or uninstall.

Anomaly Hunter is an independent tool provided "as is". It is a helper, not a guarantee of correctness; full terms of use are summarised on the Support page. You remain responsible for verifying your own data.
