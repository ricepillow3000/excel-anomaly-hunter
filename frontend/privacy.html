1What Anomaly Hunter is

Anomaly Hunter is a free Microsoft Excel add-in that scans the open sheet for likely data problems — typos, broken formulas, impossible ratios, duplicated rows and missing values — and suggests fixes. It is an independent project, offered at no charge, and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Microsoft or Google.

2Not a guarantee of correctness

Anomaly Hunter highlights values that look unusual or inconsistent. It is an assistive check, not a certification that your spreadsheet is correct.

  • It will not find every error, and it may flag values that are actually correct.
  • A suggested fix is a suggestion only; nothing is changed until you review it and click Apply.
  • It makes no professional, financial, accounting, legal, medical or engineering judgement, and using it creates no advisory relationship.
You remain solely responsible for reviewing and verifying your spreadsheet before relying on it, submitting it, or making any decision based on it. Treat Anomaly Hunter's output as a second pair of eyes, not the final word.

3Your data & privacy

Anomaly Hunter is built to keep your data on your machine.

Stays on this PC. The scan runs in a local engine at https://127.0.0.1:5055, which only answers your own computer and only the Anomaly Hunter pane. There are no analytics, no cookies and no tracking; the pane loads only its own files plus Microsoft's Office script, and its Content Security Policy blocks everything else.

Files kept locally in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnomalyHunter (all removed by AnomalyHunter.exe uninstall):

FileWhat it holds
latest-scan.csvOnly if you turn on "Save each scan for Power BI" (off by default): a plain-text copy of the last sheet you scanned plus its results. Each scan replaces it; turning the option off or uninstalling deletes it.
server.logThe engine's own error log.
ai-cacheUp to 500 finished AI answers, so the same question isn't asked twice.
gemini-key.txt, invite-code.txtYour AI key or invite code, if you added one.
ca.pem, cert.pem, key.pemThe certificate that works only for 127.0.0.1.

Column limits and strength are saved inside the workbook you scanned; the auto-fix on/off choice is saved in the pane.

Only if you turn on AI help (off until you add a Google AI key or invite code): "Ask AI" sends the column names, the row you asked about, up to five rows around it, and the reason it was flagged. "Check words with AI" sends only the distinct words from your text columns — never your rows or numbers. With your own key this goes directly to Google's Gemini API; with an invite code it passes through the project's Cloudflare relay, which keeps only a daily count and stores none of your data. Google's free tier may use what you send to improve its products, so do not ask AI about private or personal data. Turn it off with Remove key.
