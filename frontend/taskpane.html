    All fairness checks

      Rows to check

      Worst first. Click a row to see what's wrong and a suggested fix. The cells to check are coloured in your sheet. This changes no values. Remove highlights takes the colour off.

        AI looks at what words mean, so it can spot a value that doesn't belong, like a city in a product column. It sends only the different words in your text columns to Google (through John's relay if you use an invite code), never your rows or numbers. Its answers are leads to check.

          Exact limits per column (advanced)

          The slider above is the easy way. Here you can set the exact number below or above which a column gets flagged; these win over the slider for that column.

          When to flag a number

          A number below Flag below or above Flag above gets flagged. Leave a box empty for no limit.

          Suggested fix

            Or start from one of these. Picking one only fills the box: nothing is sent until you press Ask AI.

            Ask AI sends the column names, this row and up to 5 rows above and below it, the row's formulas, why it was flagged and what you type here, to Google (through John's relay with an invite code or with no key of your own). Long cells are cut to 200 characters. Up to 5 fixes you accepted before in a column with this name (the name, the kind of fix, and the old and new values) are sent only once the AI memory is switched on in a later update.

            AI help with fixes is off. Turn it on for free under AI help at the bottom.

            Options

            Auto-fix (optional)

            Only capitals/spaces slips, like "east " where the column says "East", and only when one spelling is clearly the usual one. It never changes numbers and never guesses. After it fixes something, "Undo auto-fixes" puts it back.

            Power BI (optional)

            Off: Arcas keeps no copy of your sheet. On: after each scan, Arcas saves two files on this PC for Power BI to open. One is a copy of your sheet (latest-scan.csv). The other lists the rows it flagged and the ones you fixed or dismissed (latest-flags.csv). To find them, type %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnomalyHunter in the address bar of File Explorer. Turn this off and the next scan deletes both.

            AI help (optional, free)

            You don't need this. Arcas finds and fixes problems without AI.

            1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account. No credit card.
            2. Click Create API key and copy it.
            3. Paste it here and click Save.

            Google charges for AI help only if the key's Google project has billing turned on, which means adding a payment card. Without billing, the free tier costs nothing.

            If John gave you an invite code (it starts with inv_), paste it in the same box. It uses his shared AI with a daily limit, and his key never reaches this PC.

            Google's free tier may use what AI help sends to improve its products, so don't ask AI about private data. The key is stored only on this computer.

            Ask AI remembers up to 200 fixes you accepted, on this PC and for every workbook: each one's column name, the kind of fix, and the old and new values. Undo takes a fix back out.
