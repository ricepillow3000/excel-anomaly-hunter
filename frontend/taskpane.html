          Suggested fix

            Want AI help with fixes? Turn it on for free under AI help at the bottom.

            Auto-fix (optional)

            Only capitals/spaces slips, like "east " where the column says "East" - and only when one spelling is clearly the usual one. Never numbers, never guesses. After it fixes something, "Undo auto-fixes" puts it back.

            Power BI (optional)

            Off: no copy of your sheet is kept. On: each scan saves a plain-text copy of the sheet and its results on this PC (latest-scan.csv in your AnomalyHunter folder), which Power BI opens. Turning it off deletes that copy at the next scan.
